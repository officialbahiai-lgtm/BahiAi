Privacy Policy - BahiAi

Last updated: 15 November 2026

What BahiAi is

BahiAi is a business app for order booking, dispatch tracking and accounting, used by businesses and their staff.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address. We also store the business data you enter in the app, such as orders, customers, invoices and stock items.

How we use it

Your name and email are used only to identify you, to give you access to your business account, and to let the business owner approve or manage staff. Your business data is used only to provide the app to you.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. Data is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase, and is visible only to the approved members of your own business and to the app administrators for support.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time by writing to the email below.
